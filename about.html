Our Story

Born on the streets,
built for the culture.

KRSH started in 2019 in a garage in the back streets of the city — three friends who were tired of paying hype tax for sneakers that didn't fit their lane. We wanted something real: kicks curated by people who actually live in them.

What began as a small resale operation between friends grew into a curated marketplace for underground brands, limited drops, and collaborations you won't find anywhere else. Every pair we stock has been worn, tested, and flexed by our crew before it ever hits the shelf.

No hype tax. No fake exclusivity. Just real streetwear, for real people, built by people who grew up in the culture.
