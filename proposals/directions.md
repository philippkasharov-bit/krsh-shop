# KRSH — redesign directions (hero only)

Screens: `A|B|C-desktop.png` (1440×900), `A|B|C-mobile.png` (390×844). Code: `proposals/a.html`, `b.html`, `c.html` on `feat/redesign-directions`.

## A — Night Edition
- **Thesis:** the shop as a late-night print magazine. The alley loop is a tall cover plate, and the title is set across it like a masthead. Quiet, expensive, editorial.
- **Type:** Instrument Serif (display, roman + italic) / Inter Tight (UI).
- **Palette:** ink `#0C0B0A`, bone `#ECE6DC`, dim `#9A948A`, signal red `#E1341E` (one dot, one rule).
- **Signature motion:** the video opens through a vertical slit that widens to the full plate. The title sits on top in `difference` blend.
- **Spirit:** SSENSE editorial, Aimé Leon Dore. Strongest "premium" read, but furthest from streetwear energy.

## B — Spec Sheet
- **Thesis:** the sneaker as an engineered object on a concrete lab table. The hero is the 3D Venom itself, with callout lines and a spec bar. Price and "Select size" sit in the first viewport.
- **Type:** Archivo (62% condensed for display, 100–125% for UI) / JetBrains Mono (spec data only).
- **Palette:** concrete `#D8D5CF`, ink `#141414`, mid `#6E6B66`, hot orange `#FF4F1A`.
- **Signature motion:** the model auto-rotates and can be dragged. Callout leader lines draw in. The alley video is a small grayscale field-recording inset.
- **Spirit:** Nike ACG / Arc'teryx System_A product pages. Best use of the 3D asset, and the most distinctive in a portfolio.

## C — Drop Night
- **Thesis:** the homepage is the queue for tonight's release. Full-bleed alley video, a monumental time, a live countdown and one tape band.
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display (900) / Space Grotesk.
- **Palette:** night `#0A0A0A`, chalk `#F4F1EA`, tape yellow `#FFD60A`, mute `#8C8A85`.
- **Signature motion:** a live countdown, plus a rotated hazard-tape marquee running across the frame.
- **Spirit:** Palace / Corteiz drop pages. Highest energy and closest to the current brand, but the most "genre".

## Recommendation
**B (Spec Sheet), carrying A's editorial restraint into the catalog and journal pages.** B is the only direction where the generated 3D model and the product data *are* the design, not decoration. It also differs most from the dark-neon template look the audit flagged, and its light concrete ground sets the portfolio apart from the other three dark concept sites. Across the site:
- **Catalog:** spec-table grid with hover rotation.
- **Product page:** 3D-first with callouts.
- **Checkout:** a mono "order sheet".

C is the fallback if the owner wants to keep the loud street voice.
