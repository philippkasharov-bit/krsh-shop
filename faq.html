FAQ

Everything you need to know

Orders & Shipping

How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery (1-2 business days) is available at checkout for $15.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. International shipping times vary from 7-14 business days depending on destination.

Can I track my order?

Yes. Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link via email within 24 hours.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags attached.

How do I exchange for a different size?

Start a return for your original item and place a new order for the correct size — this is the fastest way to get the right fit while stock lasts.

Are limited drops returnable?

Limited edition and final sale items are not eligible for return unless defective. This will always be noted on the product page.

Sizing

How do KRSH sizes run?

Most KRSH silhouettes run true to size. Check the size guide on each product page for brand-specific notes.

What if my size is sold out?

Join the waitlist on the product page — we'll email you the moment it's restocked, if a restock is planned.

Authenticity

Are your sneakers authentic?

Yes, 100%. Every pair is verified by our authentication team before it ships. We never stock replicas.

Where do your limited drops come from?

Direct partnerships with underground designers, small-batch manufacturers, and verified resellers we've worked with for years.
